# Prompts visuais — Episódio 01

## Prompt global
Original cinematic stylized 3D animated fruit soap opera, premium feature-animation quality, expressive anthropomorphic fruit characters, detailed fruit skin and seeds, physically based materials, cinematic lighting, clear readable acting, polished animation, vertical 9:16 framing. Keep exact character identity, proportions, colors, leaves, eyes and wardrobe continuity across every shot. No logos, watermarks or random text.

## Scene 1 — 00–08
A poor little anthropomorphic strawberry girl gently cares for her sick strawberry mother in a modest bedroom, holding her hand beside a simple bed. Intimate emotional close-up, warm dim practical light, subtle breathing and blinking, slow camera push-in. The girl wears her faded simple dress.

## Scene 2 — 08–16
In a luxurious corporate lobby, a tall arrogant anthropomorphic pineapple businessman in a tailored charcoal suit points toward the exit while the small strawberry girl stands hurt but determined. Marble floor, glass architecture, cool dramatic lighting, medium two-shot and readable gestures.

## Scene 3 — 16–24
Rainy city sidewalk at night, wet asphalt reflecting streetlights. The strawberry girl finds an elegant handbag, opens it and discovers an ornate necklace beside an old photograph. Rain particles, insert close-up of the photo, shocked expression.

## Scene 4 — 24–34
Extreme close-up of the old photograph in the strawberry girl's hands. It shows a young strawberry child beside a wealthy family in front of a grand mansion. Brief golden memory flashes, mysterious emotional atmosphere. Do not alter the protagonist's model.

## Scene 5 — 34–44
Elegant mirror transformation: the strawberry girl now wears a sophisticated deep burgundy evening dress with subtle gold jewelry. She raises her chin and takes a determined step forward. Smooth camera orbit, warm key light, natural fabric motion.

## Scene 6 — 44–55
The same strawberry girl confidently re-enters the exact same luxurious corporate lobby from scene 2. The pineapple businessman turns, recognizes her and freezes in disbelief. Shot-reverse-shot, slow push-in, mouth shapes suitable for later lip sync.

## Scene 7 — 55–60
A mysterious fruit-like silhouette stands behind a partly open door in a dark corridor. Only outline and one hand visible; face hidden. Slow camera creep, suspenseful backlight, sudden cut to black with negative space for an editor-added end card.

## Negative prompt
Malformed anatomy, extra limbs, duplicate characters, inconsistent costumes, changing fruit shapes, flickering, jitter, low-resolution textures, flat slideshow, random lettering, subtitles baked into image, watermark, logo.
